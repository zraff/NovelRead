THE HOUSE AT LOW TIDE · CHAPTER 1

Chapter one

Salt in the walls

The first thing I noticed when I came home was the salt.

It had gathered in the corners of the windows, in the seams of the kitchen tiles, along the brass handle of the front door. It made a pale map of the house, a record of all the years I had spent away.

Outside, the tide was low enough to show the black ribs of the reef. I stood at the window and tried to remember whether the garden had always leaned toward the water.
