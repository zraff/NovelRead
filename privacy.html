Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Zraff Korazon collects and uses information when you visit this website, read stories, create a reader identity, or leave a chapter comment.

Information we collect

Anonymous visitors can read published stories without creating an account. If you create a pseudonym or sign in with Google, we store the information needed to provide reader access, including your chosen display name, authentication identifier, reading progress, and comments.

Google sign-in is handled through Supabase Auth and Google OAuth. We do not request access to your Google Drive, contacts, or other private Google services. The site uses basic identity information needed to authenticate you.

How we use information

We use reader information to keep you signed in, save your reading progress, display your chosen name on comments, prevent abuse, moderate comments, and understand which books and chapters are being read.

Cookies and storage

Authentication providers may use secure session cookies or browser storage to maintain your sign-in. The site may also store preferences such as theme and text size locally on your device. We do not use advertising trackers or sell personal information.

Comments and moderation

Comments are attached to your reader identity and the chapter where they were posted. The author may hide comments or block an identity from commenting. Please do not post sensitive personal information.

Data retention and deletion

You may request deletion of your reader identity and associated comments or progress by contacting the author. Backups may take additional time to update or remove.

Third-party services

This site may use Supabase for authentication, database, and storage services; Google for optional sign-in; and Cloudflare for hosting, DNS, and security. Their services are governed by their own policies.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact Zraff Korazon through the contact method published on this website.
